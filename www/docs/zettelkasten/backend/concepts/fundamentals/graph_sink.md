# graph_sink

In a directed flow network, a sink is a designated vertex where flow exits the network.

In a maximum-flow problem, flow enters at a source and is routed through edges to a sink, subject to edge capacities and flow conservation at intermediate vertices. The sink is a chosen endpoint for the problem; it is not necessarily a vertex with no outgoing edges in the underlying graph.
